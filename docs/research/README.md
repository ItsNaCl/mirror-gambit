# Research behind the coach

Three reports gathered in October 2026 before the v3 coach was designed. Everything is paraphrased from public sources, with links. No book text.

- `coaches.md`: how the best known coaches and players teach club-level and beginner players, where they agree and where they do not.
- `improvement.md`: what the research and the coaching consensus say about adult improvement, by rating band, and which training methods fit a learn-by-doing student.
- `tools.md`: the 2026 landscape of chess training products and the technical building blocks (APIs, engines, Maia, data, licences) a personal coach can be built on.

The curriculum in `src/lib/coach/curriculum.ts` is the distilled version.
